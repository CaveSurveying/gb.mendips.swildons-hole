The survey of Swildons Hole joins two surveys made sixty years apart: about **4.2 km** of
survey legs, 121 m from top to bottom, fixed to the OS grid at the entrance by GPS.

## What's here

- **The resurvey, about 1.8 km** (2012 to 2019, led by Paul 'Footleg' Fretwell, with DistoX
  and PocketTopo): the Entrance Series, Rolling Thunder, and the streamway from the Old 40
  past the 20 ft Pot, Double Pots, Barnes Loop and Tratman's Temple to Sump 1. All of it is
  drawn, in plan and extended elevation, with about 160 cross-sections, and has passage
  walls in the 3D view.
- **W. I. Stanton's survey, about 2.4 km** (1951 to 1962): everything beyond the resurvey,
  including St Paul's, Paradise Regained, the Maypole Series, Double Trouble and Swildons 2.
  It's centreline only: no drawing and no walls.

## What isn't

Swildons Hole is about 9 km long. **Nothing beyond Swildons 2 is in this survey**: not
Swildons 3 and on to the far sumps, nor the way through to Priddy Green Sink. Past Sump 1,
the survey is Stanton's centreline from the 1950s and 60s, so the shape is right but the
detail isn't drawn.

The drawings from below the 20 ft Pot are converted from Footleg's sketches made
underground: faithful to them, but not hand-finished. The route cards are drafts.

Take a full survey and a guidebook underground, and the
[Mendip Cave Registry's entry](https://www.mcra.org.uk/registry/sitedetails.php?id=240)
for its other surveys and references.
